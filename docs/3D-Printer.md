# 3D PRINTER
### What is the 3D printer techology?

Simply put, 3D printing is a technology that creates three-dimensional solid objects by building up material layer by layer from a digital model file. It is also known as "additive manufacturing."

To better understand, we can compare it to traditional manufacturing methods:

Traditional Manufacturing (Subtractive Manufacturing): For example, sculpting. You start with a large block of raw material (like wood or metal) and remove the unwanted parts through cutting, grinding, drilling, etc., to finally achieve the desired shape.

3D Printing (Additive Manufacturing): For example, building a house with bricks. You have a design blueprint, and then you start from the foundation, laying bricks layer by layer until the entire house is constructed. 3D printing works in the same way, creating an object from scratch by adding material layer upon layer.
